Cable and broadcast advertising

Most of our clients are not famous.

They are on television anyway. Getting them there, and making the thirty seconds count when they arrive, is the whole job.

The break does not care who you are.

Ninety seconds of airtime comes apart into four lengths, and the same four lengths are sold to everybody. A national sportswear brand and a tire shop on the north side are buying the identical inventory, in the identical pod, between the identical two segments of programming.

What separates them is not the money. It is what runs in the slot.

So what runs in the slot?

Four lengths. Four different problems.

A six second spot cannot carry a story, so it carries a name and gets out. A sixty can carry a story and usually should not. Most of the failures we are asked to fix are a thirty second idea bought at fifteen, or a fifteen second idea given a minute to fill.

About us

We are a television shop.

Halftone Media is a full service commercial advertising company. We write it, cast it, shoot it, cut it, grade it, score it, clear it and place it. Everything a spot needs in order to legally run, done under one roof, by the people who will still be on the phone when the schedule moves in week three.

We work at the top of the market and we work with the tire shop on the north side, and the two get the same room, the same director and the same argument about the last four frames. A thirty second spot is thirty seconds long whoever paid for it. The budget changes what we can put in front of the camera. It does not change how hard we think about what goes in the slot.

How a spot actually gets made.

You approve a script before anybody is booked. You sit in the edit, or you do not and we send you three cuts. Legal sees it before the station does. It is encoded to each station's spec, slated, and delivered with a traffic sheet that says exactly which version runs where. Then it runs, and we watch what it does, and in week three we move it.
